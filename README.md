# Customer Churn Analysis & Customer Intelligence

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn for a subscription-based business to understand customer attrition, identify churn patterns, evaluate revenue impact, and develop data-driven customer retention recommendations.

The analysis combines customer demographics, subscription information, and support data to identify churn trends and potential risk factors.

## Business Objectives
- Measure customer churn and retention rates.
- Identify churn patterns across subscription plans, contract types, and locations.
- Analyze customer tenure and revenue impact.
- Investigate the relationship between customer support escalations and churn.
- Identify high-risk customers and recommend retention strategies.

## Key Findings
- Overall churn rate: 28.6%
- Retention rate: 71.4%
- Monthly contract churn: 55.6%
- Annual contract churn: 8.3%
- Revenue loss due to churn: approximately $73.94 per month
- Estimated Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) erosion: $2,047

The analysis highlights a substantially higher churn rate among monthly-contract customers compared with annual-contract customers.

## Business Recommendations
- Prioritize retention efforts for customers with medium and high churn risk.
- Investigate customer complaints and support escalations.
- Encourage suitable customers to move to longer-term subscription plans.
- Investigate pricing, service issues, and competitor activity.
- Develop targeted customer retention campaigns.
